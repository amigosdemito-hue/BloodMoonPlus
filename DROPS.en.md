# Blood Moon Drops

🌐 **English** · [Español](DROPS.md)

## Probability system

1. Every horde kill has a **10% chance** of dropping something (the table's `drop-chance`, which rises with difficulty) and, if it drops, rolls **1-2 items** (`rolls`).
2. Each item also has an individual `drop-chance`: simple ones always drop; powerful ones are very rare (like a pink sheep).
3. Mythics are limited to **3 copies on the whole server** (with a serial number).

| Rarity | Typical drop chance | Description |
|--------|--------------------|-------------|
| Common | 100% | Always drop |
| Uncommon | 60-90% | Almost always |
| Rare | 5-50% | Moderate probability (broken weapons, 5%) |
| Epic | 10-20% | Low probability |
| Legendary | 4-5% | Very rare |
| Mythic | 0.5-0.8% | Pink-sheep probability |

> **Enchantments:** the maximum level is **7** on any item (it is trimmed automatically).
> **Attributes:** stat bonuses are potion effects (`passives`) while the item is worn.

## Drops per boss

### Bloody Zombie King
- **Guaranteed:** XP Bottle (8-16), Diamond (3-6)
- Minimum rarity: Rare · 4-5 rolls

### Crimson Archer
- **Guaranteed:** Arrows (32-64), Diamond (2-5)
- Minimum rarity: Rare · 4-5 rolls

### Night Executioner (and all elite bosses)
- **Guaranteed:** Netherite Scrap (3-6), XP Bottle (6-12)
- Minimum rarity: Rare · 5-6 rolls

### Top-damage prize
- Epic / Legendary / Mythic only

### Survivors (end of event)
- 100% chance · 2-3 rolls per player
